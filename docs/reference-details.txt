Description

I'm the co-founder and CEO of Jetron Ticket, where I lead growth, customer acquisition and relationships with event organizers.

What Jetron Ticket does
IN THE APP
Jetron Moments

Post moments as they happen at the event, and see the night through everyone who was there.

FOR ORGANISERS
Jetron Fluid

Sell tickets on your own website while Jetron Ticket powers everything behind the scenes.

PICK YOUR SPOT
Seat maps

Choose your exact seat from a live map of the venue.

BIG ONSALES
Queues & FairPass

Fast, fair waiting rooms for high-demand drops, with checks that keep bots out.

KNOW THE VIBE
Music previews

Listen to the headliners and DJs before you buy, so you know the energy to expect.

AT THE DOOR
Offline tickets

Your tickets open even with no network, so getting in is never the stressful part.

NEAR YOU
Events map

See what's happening around you on an interactive map, from concerts to pop-ups.

GET PAID
Wallet payouts

Organisers get their money whenever they're ready, straight from the Jetron Ticket wallet.
